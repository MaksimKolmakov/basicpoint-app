What you get

Three products. One connected ledger.

Most companies run finance across disconnected spreadsheets, where the model, the budget and the accounts never quite match. Basic Point replaces them with three products that share the same data, so a change in the plan flows all the way through to the close.

Product 01

Financial Modeling

Build the plan, and see the whole business respond.

Set your assumptions, such as pricing, volumes, headcount and costs, and Basic Point turns them into a live profit & loss, balance sheet and cash flow. Change one driver and every statement recalculates, so you can test a decision and see its effect on cash and profit before you commit to it.

  • Driver-based three-statement model that always ties out.
  • Compare scenarios side by side, from base case to downside.
  • Unit economics, runway and cash impact calculated for you.

Product 02

Budgeting

Turn the plan into a budget every team owns.

Cascade targets from the model, collect input from each department, and lock a version everyone works to. Approvals and a full history mean the number in the board deck is the number in the system, and re-forecasting through the year does not mean rebuilding the model from scratch.

  • Top-down targets meet bottom-up input from each owner.
  • Approvals, version control and a complete audit trail.
  • Rolling re-forecasts that reuse the same drivers.

Product 03

Management Accounts

Close the month, and report with confidence.

Record actuals, reconcile them against the budget, and produce a profit & loss, balance sheet and cash flow your board and investors can trust. Variances are explained line by line, and multiple entities and currencies are consolidated automatically, so the close is fast and the story behind every number is clear.

  • IFRS-aligned statements with a clean, auditable trail.
  • Budget versus actual variance, explained line by line.
  • Multi-entity consolidation with automatic currency conversion.

How it works

One flow, from plan to close.

The three products are stages of the same process. Because they share one ledger, nothing is re-keyed and nothing drifts out of sync between them.

Step 1 · Plan

Model the business

Set your drivers and build the forecast. This becomes the single plan the whole company works from.

↓ feeds the budget
Step 2 · Commit

Budget and approve

Turn the plan into an owned, approved budget by department, and re-forecast as the year unfolds.

↓ sets the target
Step 3 · Close

Report the actuals

Record what happened, reconcile against the budget, and publish management accounts that tie back to the plan.

↻ informs the next plan
